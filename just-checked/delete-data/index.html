Delete your data from Just Checked

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Just Checked has no account and keeps your items on your phone. This page explains how to delete what the app keeps, and what is kept elsewhere.

Delete everything on your phone

1. Open Just Checked. 2. Open Settings (the gear at the top). 3. Scroll to Your data and tap Delete my data. 4. Tap Delete everything.

This deletes every item, every tap, your settings and all reminders, from the app and from the home-screen widget, at once. It cannot be undone. Uninstalling the app also deletes all of it from your phone.

Data kept outside your phone

  • Purchases (Plus). Google Play and RevenueCat keep the record of a purchase under an anonymous id. Google keeps its own record under your Google account. To ask us to delete the RevenueCat record, write to support@laterals.app from any address and say which purchase it was (the order number from your Google Play receipt). We delete it within 30 days; Google keeps what tax and payment laws require.
  • Crash reports. If crash reporting is on in your version of the app, Sentry keeps reports for up to 90 days and then deletes them. They hold no name and no item list. Write to us if you want them deleted sooner.
