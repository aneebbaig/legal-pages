Privacy policy for Just Checked

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Just Checked ("the app") is made by Mirza Aneeb Baig, an independent developer in Karachi, Pakistan ("we", "us"). We are responsible for the data described here. Contact for anything about your data: support@laterals.app.

The short version

  • There is no account. You do not give us your name or email.
  • Your items, taps and settings stay on your phone.
  • We do not run ads and we do not use analytics.
  • Two outside services get limited data: crash reports (Sentry) and purchases (RevenueCat, through Google Play).

What stays on your phone

The names you give your items, when you tap them, their colors and order, due times and reminder choices, and your settings (vibration, theme, when your day starts). The home-screen widget reads a copy of this on the same phone. We do not receive any of it.

Delete it at any time: Settings, "Delete my data" removes all items, taps and settings from the app and the widget and cancels reminders. Uninstalling the app also removes it from the phone. Android may include the app's files in your own Google backup, which Google keeps under your account and settings, not ours.

What leaves your phone

Crash reports. If the app crashes, it can send a crash report to Sentry (sentry.io) so we can fix the bug. We turn off Sentry's default personal data, screenshots and screen-structure capture, and we do not send performance data. A report holds technical details such as the error, the app version and basic device information (the exact device fields are set by Sentry's software). We do not put your item names in reports on purpose. In rare cases a name could still appear inside an error's text. If crash reporting is not switched on in your version of the app, nothing is sent.

The optional on-device models. Under "On-device AI" in settings you can choose a model that fills in a new item from a note in your own words. Gemma 4 (about 2.6 GB) and Qwen3 (about 345 MB) are downloaded from Hugging Face (huggingface.co); like any download, Hugging Face sees your phone's IP address, and nothing you type is sent. Gemini Nano is Google's model built into some phones; Android provides it, and the app uses it on the phone. Every model runs on your phone with no connection, and what you type never leaves it. You can remove each one in settings.

Purchases. If you buy Plus, Google Play handles the payment; we never see your card details. We use RevenueCat (revenuecat.com) to check whether Plus is active. The app gives RevenueCat no name or email; RevenueCat creates an anonymous id for the app on your phone and receives your purchase information from Google Play. See RevenueCat's and Google's privacy policies for what they keep: RevenueCat and Google.

The app also stores on your phone that Plus is on, so reminders and the widget work offline.

What we do not collect

Account details, contacts, location, photos, microphone, camera, advertising id, or analytics about how you use the app.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show your reminders.
  • Alarms and reminders (exact alarms): to show reminders on the minute. You can refuse; reminders then arrive a little late.
  • Vibrate: for touch feedback and reminders. You can switch it off in settings.
  • Run at startup: to restore reminders and the widget after you restart your phone.
  • Internet: for crash reports and purchases.

Health information

You may name items after medicines or health routines. That text stays on your phone (see above). The app is not a medical device and gives no medical advice.

Children

The app is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Your choices and rights

Use "Delete my data" in settings. For data held by Google Play or RevenueCat, contact them, or contact us at support@laterals.app and we will help where we can. Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, the UK or California) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or move your data and to object to how it is used. Because we hold no account and your items stay on your phone, most of these you can do yourself in the app; for anything else write to us and we will answer within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above and, for material changes, say so in the app's release notes.
