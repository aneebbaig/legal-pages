Privacy policy for SalahSeed

Last updated: 20 November 2026

SalahSeed ("the app") is made by Mirza Aneeb Baig, an independent developer in Karachi, Pakistan ("we", "us"). We are responsible for the data described here. Contact for anything about your data: support@anhib.com.

The short version

  • There is no account. You do not give us your name or email.
  • Your prayers, your garden, your qaza count, your place and your settings stay on your phone.
  • We do not run ads and we do not use analytics.
  • Two outside services get limited data: crash reports (Sentry) and tips (RevenueCat, through Google Play).

What stays on your phone

Which prayers you marked as prayed, missed, made up or exempt, your garden and streak, your qaza count, the place you chose, the calculation method and Asr school, your alarm and adhan choices, your language and your other settings. We do not receive any of it.

Your place. Prayer times and the sky are calculated on your phone for the place you choose. You can search for a city (the list is inside the app, no connection needed) or tap "Use where I am". Only then does the app ask Android for your approximate location, once, and finds the nearest city on the phone. Your location is never sent to us or anyone else.

Delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android's settings, or by uninstalling the app. Android may include the app's files in your own Google backup, which Google keeps under your account and settings, not ours.

What leaves your phone

Crash reports. If the app crashes, it can send a crash report to Sentry (sentry.io) so we can fix the bug. We turn off Sentry's default personal data, screenshots and screen-structure capture, we do not store IP addresses, and we do not send performance data. A report holds technical details such as the error, the app version and basic device information (the exact device fields are set by Sentry's software). It holds no place and no prayer record. If crash reporting is not switched on in your version of the app, nothing is sent.

Tips. The whole app is free. If you choose to leave a tip, Google Play handles the payment; we never see your card details. We use RevenueCat (revenuecat.com) to sell the tip. The app gives RevenueCat no name or email; RevenueCat creates an anonymous id for the app on your phone and receives your purchase information from Google Play. See RevenueCat's and Google's privacy policies for what they keep: RevenueCat and Google.

Shake to report. If you shake the phone and choose to report a problem, the app opens an email draft in your own email app, addressed to us, with the app version and device model. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself.

What we do not collect

Account details, contacts, precise location, photos, microphone, camera, advertising id, or analytics about how you use the app.

Permissions

  • Approximate location (optional): only when you tap "Use where I am", to find your city. You can search for a city instead.
  • Notifications: to show prayer alarms and the reminder before a prayer's time ends.
  • Alarms and reminders (exact alarms): to sound the alarm at the start of each prayer, on the minute.
  • Vibrate: for touch feedback and alarms.
  • Run at startup: to restore alarms after you restart your phone.
  • Internet: for crash reports and tips.

Children

The app is meant for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone, and we collect nothing that identifies a child.

Your choices and rights

Clear the app's storage or uninstall it (how to delete your data). For data held by Google Play or RevenueCat, contact them, or contact us at support@anhib.com and we will help where we can. Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, the UK or California) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or move your data and to object to how it is used. Because we hold no account and your records stay on your phone, most of these you can do yourself; for anything else write to us and we will answer within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above and, for material changes, say so in the app's release notes.
