Delete your data from SalahSeed

Last updated: 20 November 2026

SalahSeed has no account and keeps your prayer records on your phone. This page explains how to delete what the app keeps, and what is kept elsewhere.

Delete everything on your phone

  1. Open your phone's Settings, then Apps, then SalahSeed.
  2. Tap Storage and then Clear storage (or Clear data).

This deletes your prayer records, garden, qaza count, place and settings, and cancels the alarms. It cannot be undone. Uninstalling the app also deletes all of it from your phone.

Data kept outside your phone

  • Tips. Google Play and RevenueCat keep the record of a tip under an anonymous id. Google keeps its own record under your Google account. To ask us to delete the RevenueCat record, write to support@anhib.com from any address and say which purchase it was (the order number from your Google Play receipt). We delete it within 30 days; Google keeps what tax and payment laws require.
  • Crash reports. If crash reporting is on in your version of the app, Sentry keeps reports for up to 90 days and then deletes them. They hold no name, no place and no prayer record. Write to us if you want them deleted sooner.
